## Design Context

### Users
Candidates and students preparing for competitive interviews, technical assessments, and professional roles. They need a distraction-free, highly functional environment to practice and improve their skills.

### Brand Personality
Ambitious, Practical, Empowering.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Vibe**: Technical, functional, and competitive (inspired by LeetCode, HackerRank, Codeforces, Forage) but with a more premium, modern, and engaging execution.
**Theme**: Maintaining the current "Deep Tech" dark theme (Deep Tech Blue background, Glass Cards, Neon Indigo/Purple accents). Keeping the vibrant, existing dark mode colors rather than shifting to an overly dark or pure-black aesthetic.
**Typography**: Moving beyond default `Inter` to incorporate distinctive, bold typefaces (e.g., geometric or modernized technical fonts for headings) while maintaining high readability for rigorous practice sessions.

### Design Principles
1. **Gamified Achievement**: The visual design must instill a strong sense of achievement and progress. Success states, levels, and completed drills should be rewarded through intentional success colors and satisfying micro-interactions.
2. **Dense but Decisive Layouts**: Like top competitive programming platforms, screens will often be data-rich. The design must handle density gracefully without feeling cluttered, using crisp typography and tight spacing rather than wrapping everything in heavy card containers.
3. **Targeted Neon Flourishes**: Let the deep tech background sit quietly, using the primary and secondary neon colors strictly to guide attention, highlight primary actions, and reward the user. Avoid using glassmorphism or glow on every single element.
4. **Purposeful Tech Aesthetics**: Lean into the "ambitious developer" identity through sophisticated typography and layout, avoiding generic "AI slop" decorations. Every design choice must feel like a deliberate, premium tool for the user's success.
